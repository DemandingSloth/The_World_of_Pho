business district. 

Player can have 6/13 businesses active at any one time. They can only Add to active stores pools. must wait 72 hours after switching a store to switch it again. 

Active stores provide bonuses for threshholds met. So a player deposits items that the shop accepts. it increases the shops inventory. Starting at 50k deposits (this is intended for later game when your overflowing with items.)

13 - 550,000,000
14 - 750,000,000
15 - 1,000,000,000

At level 15 that bonus becomes permanent and no longer counts towards your 6/13 active bonuses. so if you have two shops maxed, you'll be able to have up to 8 bonuses active at one time.
